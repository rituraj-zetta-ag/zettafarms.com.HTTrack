Extension Enablers

Individuals interested in partnering with us for farming, expanding their agri-business, or establishing agri-processing units can connect with us through our extension enabler model.

What are the minimum requirements?

1. Land Area: At least 100 acres, can be in multiple patches within 2 to 3 km.
2. Proper Irrigation system
3. Fencing around the farm
4. Electricity connection
5. Road Connectivity to the Farm
6. Storage and Stay facility

These requirements may be adjusted based on geographical locations.
