Maize: maize farming, maize health benefits, and sweet corn

Maize is also known as ‘corn’ in different parts of the world. The scientific name of maize is Zea mays. Maize plants belong to the family Poaceae. This is a great staple food, maize in Hindi, known as Makki.

Maize and maize products are the backbone of many healthy products in the world. It is gluten-free, while its dietary fiber content contributes to digestive health. Native Americans and North Americans come on top while consuming corn.

Introduction

The maize plant is grassy and one of the most cultivated staple foods in the world. Corn is a famous cereal grain widely used across the world. Maize originated in Central America and southern Mexico.

The plant has a tall, green stalk along with its broad leaves. These flowers contain male reproductive organs on top of the flower as well.

Many youngsters like to have boiled corn on the cob because it’s good for digestive health.

Top maize-producing states

Madhya Pradesh and Karnataka come first in maize-producing states. Bihar is the second-largest maize-producing state. Andhra Pradesh has the highest state productivity.

Maize Farming

This farming, like any other agricultural endeavor, also faces challenges. Maize plants are also prone to diseases. The plants face diseases like corn smut and northern corn leaf blight. These diseases will affect the yields of the plants.

  • Land Preparation: Maize cultivation needs a good land area without any weeds. The maize plant needs a firm seedbed that is free from any weeds. The land should be plowed well. Followed by harrowing, which brings soil to the tilth.
  • Soil: The maize trees grow well in loamy sand or sandy soil. The soil should have water-holding capacity. Maize plants grow well at neutral pH levels. Maize is a sensitive plant. The pH range of the soil is 5.5 to 7.5, and proper drainage is mandatory.
  • Climate: The maize plant can withstand any kind of frost at any stage of growth. In India, the cultivation extends from the hot, arid places. Corn grows in states like Rajasthan and Gujarat, as well as in wet hilly areas like Assam and Bengal.
  • Temperature: Maize grows where the night temperature does not go below 15.60 °C. It cannot withstand frost at any stage of its growth.
  • Irrigation: After planting the corn seeds, it’s important to water the plant right away. The first irrigation should happen on the third or fourth day, depending on the soil. During the initial 25 to 30 days, the corn crop does not necessitate additional water supply. It’s best to water it less often during this time, maybe just a week.
  • Diseases and Insects: The common diseases in maize farming are downy mildew and leaf spot.
  • Downy mildew: To control the downy mildew infection, remove the affected plants. Spraying Metalaxyl 72 WP for downy mildew
  • Leaf spot: Spray captain or Mancozeb per acre when the infection is high among plants.
  • Fertilizer: Farm Yard Manure application should be made during the preparation of the land. It should apply inorganic fertilizers like superphosphate, urea, and potash as a base dose. After 20 days of seed sowing, farmers should apply urea, and then after 40 days, apply potash as the top dressing.

Maize Types/ Types of corn

Maize Tree

Maize is a cereal plant that belongs to the grass family. The maize plant starts as a seed. As it matures, it develops into tall stalks with leaves, and the cob contains the kernels.

Maize seed

Maize seeds are also known as kernels, and they come in different colors like yellow, white, and some shades of blue as well. Maize seed is also used as livestock feed, and it contributes to the global agricultural ecosystem.

Harvesting

The harvesting should be done when the outer cover of the crop turns from green to white.

Season

Maize is grown in all three seasons in India. The Rabi crop grows from October to November, and the spring crop grows from January to February. Kharif crop is grown in June and July.

Baby Corn

Baby corn is essentially young maize and is harvested before it reaches maturity. These mini corns are tender, sweet, and widely used in different cuisines.

Maize benefits

Among the healthy grains, It stands out as a unique, versatile cereal grain that is gluten-free. Maize flour is an alternate option for those who are allergic to gluten. Countries like the United States use its multiple resources in their daily diet.

Maize uses and food products

Corn is a great staple food among fruits and vegetables across the globe. Today, manufacturers have started producing different processed foods from corn and its extracts.

Maize products:

From maize, various food products are prepared, like corn oil, corn flour, corn syrup, and popcorn. In different cuisines, baby corn is an essential ingredient.

  1. Popcorn: Popcorn is made by heating the corn kernels. It’s a good source of fiber; to improve its taste, add some butter or season it with different flavors.
  2. Corn flour: The corn flour is made from finely ground corn kernels. It’s a gluten-free flour, and it’s a great alternative to wheat flour. Corn flour is used in many recipes to thicken the dishes. Corn flour is a great food option for people who suffer from gluten allergies and celiac diseases.
  3. Corn syrup: It is used as a sweetener, derived from corn starch. Corn syrup is used to prepare candies for baked goods. Corn syrup plays a crucial role in enhancing sweetness and adding flavor to food products.
  4. Corn oil: Corn oil is one of the main products extracted from corn extracts. It is mostly used as a cooking oil, and it has a mild flavor with a high smoke point. It’s a great option for baking and frying food items. It is loaded with heart-healthy nutrients such as saturated fats; it’s healthy and nutritious by nature.

Ans: Various varieties of corn and maize exist, including sweet corn and popcorn.

3. What is the difference between regular corn and sweet corn?

Ans: Field corn is often called regular corn, and it’s used for animal feed and industrial purposes.

Sweet corn has a higher sugar content, and it gets sweeter and tender when it’s cooked.
